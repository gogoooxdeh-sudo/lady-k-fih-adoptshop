01 · The shop

What AdoptShop is

Lady K ★ Fih — AdoptShop is a boutique for original character designs. Premade Adopts are individually listed designs; Custom Adopts are requests for a personalized concept and artist quotation, not items already in stock.

Listings and request statuses are maintained by the artist. Submitting a request does not complete a purchase, transfer ownership or authorize use of the design.

02 · Premade designs

How premade adopts work

Open an Available character to review its Adopt ID, category, description, included-file notes, images and base price. Select one of the listed license choices and submit a request with your name, email and Discord username. No buyer account is needed.

When a purchase request is accepted for review, the adopt may be reserved while payment is arranged. Approval is separate from payment, and a cancelled request must be explicitly returned to the shop before the adopt becomes available again.

03 · License choices

04 · Premade request and payment

Purchase process

The artist reviews the request and, if proceeding, provides payment instructions separately. Payments are checked and confirmed manually; submitting a request does not charge you or transfer money.

Approval is not payment or delivery. A premade adopt is marked Sold and moved to the archive only after payment is confirmed and delivery is completed. Ownership records and certificates are then prepared for review.

The payment method and all actual payment conditions must be checked in the official Terms and with Lady K ★ Fih before paying.

05 · Personalized designs

How Custom Adopts work

Describe the character, category, appearance, colors, clothing, personality, references and preferred license. The artist decides whether to accept the request, decline it or request clarification, and determines any quotation manually.

The administration panel tracks an initial 60% payment and a final 40% payment against the quoted total, including the selected license add-on. The artist begins the design after manually confirming the initial payment. These are tracking controls only; no payment is collected.

There is no fixed numerical revision limit. Each client suggestion is recorded for individual review; the artist may accept or decline it according to the official ToS. Final approval is recorded separately after a design preview is presented.

06 · Delivery and certificates

Final files and certificate previews

Final files and the selected license are delivered only after the final payment is manually confirmed. The administrator records delivery after the files have actually been sent. The shop does not send files or customer messages automatically.

Certificates are created for artist review after the required payment and delivery conditions are met. They are artist-issued ownership records and do not replace the official Terms of Service. Ownership history is kept in the administrator panel and is not displayed on public pages.

07 · Answers

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to request a character?

No account is required to submit a request. Full Name, Email Address and Discord Username are required on both request forms.

Does an approved request mean the adopt is sold?

No. Approval and payment confirmation are separate. The inventory becomes Sold only after the explicit manual payment-confirmation step.

Does a reservation expire by itself?

No. Reservations do not expire automatically. The administrator must release them manually.

Can every revision suggestion be required?

No. Each suggestion is reviewed individually by the artist under the official Terms of Service; every suggestion is reviewed individually under the official Terms of Service.

Do certificates replace the official Terms of Service?

No. The official Terms of Service remain the legal source of truth for the license and permitted uses.

Are my reference images uploaded?

Reference images are uploaded securely when the request is submitted, subject to the stated file limits.

Where do license rights and payment terms come from?

From the complete official Terms of Service, not this summary. Review the linked ToS and contact Lady K ★ Fih with questions before payment.
